# 🚀 Cobra Fee Analyzer - Quick Start Guide

## 5-Minute Setup

### Step 1: Upload Your Statement
1. Open the Cobra Fee Analyzer app
2. Click "Choose File" or drag your .xlsx statement file
3. Wait for automatic processing (usually 5-10 seconds)

### Step 2: Review Key Metrics
- **Total Fees**: Your trading costs for the period
- **Net P&L**: Your profit/loss after fees
- **Most Expensive Symbol**: Your costliest stock to trade

### Step 3: Analyze Performance
- **Check Symbol Performance**: See your best/worst performing symbols
- **Review Risk Analysis**: Click cards to see problematic trades
- **Use Interactive Charts**: Drill down into fee categories

## 🎯 Key Features at a Glance

| Feature | What It Shows | How to Use |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| **Daily Fee Breakdown** | Daily fee trends + P&L line | View chart to spot patterns |
| **Interactive Fee Breakdown** | Drill-down pie chart | Click slices to explore by symbol/date |
| **Risk Analysis Cards** | Problem areas | Click cards to see detailed positions |
| **Transaction Table** | All your trades | Use filters to focus on specific data |
| **Export Functions** | Download your data | CSV/Excel export buttons |

## 🔍 Quick Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "No data found" | Check file format (.xlsx from Cobra) |
| Chart not showing | Wait for processing to complete |
| Wrong totals | Verify statement date range |
| Missing transactions | Ensure complete statement export |

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Filter by date**: Use "2024-01" format in date filter
- **Export filtered data**: Apply filters before exporting
- **Check risk cards**: Click to see which trades need attention
- **Compare symbols**: Use performance metrics to optimize trading

## 🔒 Privacy Note
All data stays in your browser - nothing is sent to servers.
